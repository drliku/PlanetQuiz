# Planet Quiz

How well do you know the Solar System? Answer against the clock and beat your best score.

- Easy (20s), Medium (15s) and Hard (12s) modes, each with its own question set
- 10 random questions per round
- Kahoot-style scoring: an instant answer is worth 1,000 points, dropping to 500 at the buzzer
- Streak bonus of +100 per answer in a row (up to +500)
- Top 5 high scores per difficulty, saved in your browser
- Animated planets, confetti and sound effects

## Play

No build step. Open `index.html` in a browser, or serve the folder:

```sh
python3 -m http.server
```

Keys: `1`–`4` to answer, `Esc` to quit.

# Element Match

Flip the cards and match each chemical symbol with its element in as few moves as you can.

- Two modes: **Hide cards** (flip and remember, with a quick peek first) or **Just match** (all cards face up)
- Easy (6 pairs), Medium (8 pairs) and Hard (10 pairs, Latin symbols like Fe, Au and Pb)
- Kahoot-style scoring: an instant match is worth 1,000 points, dropping to 500 as the speed bar drains; the bar refills after every match
- Combo bonus of +100 per match in a row (up to +500) and a fun fact for every match
- 1–3 stars per round and top 5 best scores per mode and level, saved in your browser

Open `element-match/index.html` to play.

# Neuron Maze

Guide a signal through a maze of neurons to the brain before it fades.

- Easy, Medium and Hard levels with bigger mazes and longer timers; 3 fresh random mazes per run
- Kahoot-style scoring: reaching the brain instantly is worth 1,000 points, dropping to 500 at the time limit; a faded signal scores 0
- Grab ✦ sparks for +3 seconds and +100 points
- Arrow keys or WASD to move, on-screen pad for touch, swipe to dash down a corridor
- Top 5 best scores per level, saved in your browser

Open `neuron-maze/index.html` to play.
